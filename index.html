01 / about

About me

I'm Nishen (also Madawa), a BSc undergraduate in Cybersecurity & Cloud Computing at Sri Lanka Technological Campus (SLTC) Research University. My thing is the blue team — monitoring, detection and response — the discipline of a Security Operations Center.

I don't learn security from slides. I stand up real labs on AWS, break and defend them, and play CTFs across web, OSINT, forensics and crypto. My goal: become a capable security professional who can also ship secure web products and earn independently.

This site leans into that. It's themed as a SOC — and over the coming days it'll literally start watching how you interact with it. Friendly, never creepy.
